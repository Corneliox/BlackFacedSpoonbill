## Research Scope & Collaboration Context

This project forms an ongoing technological contribution to the ecological conservation and monitoring of the endangered **Black-faced Spoonbill (*Platalea minor*)**, focusing on coastal wetland habitats across western and southwestern Taiwan (including the Chiku, Qigu, and Tainan wetland estuaries).

The developed pipeline combines state-of-the-art instance segmentation (YOLO11), custom coordinate attention mechanisms (BeakAttention @ P3), Sliced Aided Hyper Inference (SAHI), and standalone cross-platform applications to support both field ecological researchers and automated survey installations.
